Rent Agreement Maker

The AI-powered app for creating professional rental and lease agreements directly from your iPhone — in minutes, not hours. Scan existing agreements to review their completeness offline.

Download for iOS
Overview

Paperwork, simplified.

Rent Agreement Maker replaces messy templates and manual formatting with a clean, structured workflow — pick a property type, apply AI-suggested clauses, and export a polished agreement ready to sign. Built for landlords, property managers, agents, tenants, and short-term rental hosts alike.

Features

Built for a smooth workflow.

AI Clause Suggestions

Smart clause recommendations based on your property type.

Works Offline

No internet connection or account required to get started.

Multi-Channel Export

Export PDFs directly via WhatsApp, Email, or AirDrop.

Clause Library

Toggle clauses for pet policy, parking, utilities, and more.

Rent Increase Calculator

Automatically calculate rent increases within your agreement.

Renewal Reminders

Get notified ahead of lease renewals and rent due dates.
