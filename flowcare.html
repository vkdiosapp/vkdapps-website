FlowCare

A private, respectful period tracker and cycle companion. Engineered with zero third-party telemetry, on-device encryption, ovulation alerts, and gentle health observations.

Android & iOS — Launching 2026 Inquire About Early Access
Privacy Commitment

Your intimate health data belongs solely to you.

Many health apps commodify intimate user metrics by selling information to advertising brokers and third-party trackers. FlowCare was built in response: an encrypted, local-first cycle tracking experience with zero advertising and zero telemetry.

Key Specifications

Platforms: iOS & Android (Flutter)
Encryption: AES-256 on-device key management
Data Privacy: Zero cloud trackers or external brokers
Status: Active Development
Design Features

Thoughtful, discreet, and reliable

Cycle Calendar

Clear visual layout showing past periods, predicted fertile windows, and future dates.

Zero Data Leakage

Data never leaves your phone. Protected by device biometrics (FaceID & Fingerprint).

Discreet Notifications

Customizable reminder copy that protects your privacy on locked mobile screens.

Gentle Health Insights

Symptom correlation graphs highlighting mood, energy, and cycle regularity patterns.

No Ads Ever

We never show advertising or monetize your health data through marketing brokers.

Private Backup Option

Encrypted local JSON backups exportable directly to your private iCloud or Google Drive.
