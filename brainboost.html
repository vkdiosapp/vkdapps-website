BrainBoost

A daily training ground for your mind. BrainBoost combines memory games, IQ challenges, reflex tests, and logic puzzles into short, focused sessions powered by adaptive AI.

Overview

Training that adapts to you.

BrainBoost uses adaptive difficulty to keep every session challenging but achievable, tracking your progress across memory, logic, reflex, and focus over time — so improvement is visible, not just implied.

A complete mental workout.

Memory Games

Sharpen recall with progressively challenging memory exercises.

IQ Challenges

Test and build reasoning skills with curated IQ-style puzzles.

Reflex Games

Quick-response challenges that train speed and reaction time.

Logic Games

Pattern and logic puzzles that build structured thinking.

Focus Training

Exercises specifically designed to strengthen sustained attention.

Daily Challenges

A fresh, curated challenge every day to build a consistent habit.
