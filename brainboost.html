BrainBoost

32+ AI-powered games and puzzles across Reaction, Memory, Math, Visual, and Logic — plus an AI Coach that turns your play history into a personalized training plan.

Overview

Training that adapts to you.

BrainBoost tracks every session across five categories, then hands the data to an AI Coach that surfaces personalized reports and recommendations — so improvement is visible, not just implied. Four puzzle games with 2,100+ levels round out the experience.

A complete mental workout.
