A casual spin wheel game packed with mini games, math challenges, and party fun. Create custom wheels for daily decisions, or jump into multiplayer Spin Battles with friends.

Overview

Decisions, without the debate.

Whether you're picking a restaurant, settling an argument, or running a multiplayer battle with friends, PickSpin gives you a fast, fair, and genuinely satisfying way to decide — wrapped in an interface that feels premium rather than gimmicky.

Decision Spinner

Create fully customizable wheels for daily decisions and challenges.

Math Challenge

Practice math by spinning the wheel and solving questions on the fly.

Race to 10

Custom Wheels

Build and save your own spinner wheels for any decision.
